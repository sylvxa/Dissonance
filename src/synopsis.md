# Dissonance

...is one more Discord <-> Minecraft linking mod. It supports modding platforms (currently NeoForge and Fabric), has a ton of configuration options, and is always open to new features!

[![GitHub repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/github-repo-blue?logo=github&logoColor=cdd6f4&labelColor=181825&style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/sylvxa/.mdDissonance)
[![Discord server](https://img.shields.io/discord/1282059964935831664?logo=discord&logoColor=cdd6f4&label=discord&labelColor=181825&style=for-the-badge)](https://discord.com/invite/5qzrB98sBA)

Look at the [setup documentation](./setup/base_configuration.md) for how to get Dissonance working.

Click the <i class="fas fa-font"></i> button in the top-right menu to toggle the more "on-brand" font, if that's what you're into.
